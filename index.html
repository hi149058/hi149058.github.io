Frisk

A personal voice assistant that runs on one student's Mac.

Say "Hey Frisk" and ask what's due this week, add a task, check your calendar, start a study doc, or ask about something on your screen. Frisk pulls school assignments, Google Tasks, Google Calendar and Gmail into one place and answers out loud, with an on-screen panel for lists and links.

How it works

  • The wake word, speech-to-text and voice all run locally on the Mac.
  • Questions are answered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.
  • Google access is used only to read and organize the owner's own school work.

Frisk is a personal project, not a public product: it connects only to its owner's own Google account. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses.
